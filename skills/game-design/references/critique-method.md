# Critique Method

Critique the relationship between intention, system, and experienced result.
Do not critique Robert's worth, seriousness, or potential.

## Establish the target

Restate:

- intended player experience;
- current design question;
- target audience and context, if known;
- evidence available;
- what remains deliberately out of scope.

If the target is missing, ask for it or make the uncertainty visible rather
than judging against an invented standard.

## Trace the causal chain

```text
rule or affordance
  -> player information and incentive
  -> likely action or strategy
  -> system response
  -> feedback and interpretation
  -> repeated behavior
  -> possible experience
```

Name where the chain is supported, inferred, contradicted, or untested.

## Review consequential seams

Choose the few that matter:

- intention-mechanic alignment;
- meaningful choice and agency;
- information and feedback;
- challenge, failure, and recovery;
- progression and reward pressure;
- dominant or degenerate strategies;
- pacing and cognitive load;
- accessibility and clarity;
- narrative-system coherence;
- social dynamics;
- manipulation, gambling-like mechanics, or monetization pressure;
- prototype fitness for the question.

## Deliver useful critique

Use this shape:

```text
Intended effect:
Observed or predicted effect:
Evidence state:
Strongest design seam:
Why it matters:
Smallest revision:
What the revision may sacrifice:
Next test:
```

Preserve strengths that are serving the intention. Avoid redesigning the whole
game to demonstrate expertise.

## Handle disagreement

If Robert rejects a critique, clarify whether the disagreement concerns the
intended experience, causal prediction, evidence, taste, or acceptable
trade-off. Preserve the disagreement when unresolved. Do not convert agent
confidence into authority.

## Close with learning

Separate:

- what the design may have taught;
- what Robert may have learned, using only his supported account;
- what the agent should consider changing;
- what remains unknown until play.
