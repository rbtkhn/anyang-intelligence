# Singularity Science

Say `singularity` to activate this umbrella workflow.

This skill governs the transformation of available source material into an auditable, forward-looking research system. It covers source intake, archive governance, lane routing, signal detection, mechanism extraction, decision compression, retention, primitive testing, reconciliation, and controlled downstream handoff.

It is activated only by an operator command or a clearly requested Singularity Science workflow. A mention of “singularity” inside source content is not an activation request.

## Core law

`project lane -> archive lane -> lane templates -> ledger -> signal/decision receipt`

Singularity Science is advisory. It may surface research signals, governance warnings, reusable primitive candidates, and guarded decisions. It may not independently authorize customer action, publication, rights clearance, legal or safety decisions, or transfer of transcript bulk across membranes.

## Scope and boundaries

Use this skill when source material is already materially present and the work concerns one or more of:

- governed archive intake;
- source-to-decision analysis;
- source clustering and recurrence;
- forward-looking signal or mechanism review;
- retention and revisit management;
- primitive candidate or primitive-test preparation;
- intake validation, reconciliation, or value measurement;
- approved translation into a lane-safe research receipt.

Do not fetch sources, create an unapproved lane, establish rights certainty, publish outward-facing doctrine, route transcript bulk, or turn source claims into Anyang Intelligence claims.

Keep specialized workflows separate. Delegate World Monitor review, reality checking, recurrence review, learning updates, primitive preservation, and customer-lane implementation to their governing skills when required.

## Intake tiers

Choose the tier using consequence, novelty, recurrence, sensitivity, intended transfer value, and processing cost.

- **Light:** provenance, rights status, short source note, initial interpretation, and disposition.
- **Standard:** the full Decision Compression section, lane analysis, evidence gaps, and canonical ledger update.
- **Deep:** standard intake plus independent corroboration, recurrence analysis, sensitive-domain review, rights review, and downstream receipt preparation.

All tiers retain the same archive packet shape and no-fetch boundary. Default to Standard when uncertain.

## Required archive workflow

1. Confirm the source body is materially present.
2. Select and explain an existing archive lane.
3. Read the project README, archive README, lane README, source-note template, analysis template, and canonical ledger.
4. Assign a stable `source_id`; use one cluster or mechanism ID when multiple sources support the same signal.
5. Create exactly one transcript with attribution, URL or explicit unavailable status, rights status, capture method, and no invented publication date. Redact direct contact strings unless explicitly approved.
6. Create exactly one conforming source note.
7. Create exactly one conforming analysis.
8. Update only the canonical lane ledger.
9. Add signal, mechanism, retention, or downstream receipt links when the analysis supports them.
10. Stop at the selected tier’s boundary and report what remained inside Singularity Science.

Source notes and analyses must be original synthesis, not transcript-paraphrase dumps.

## Source-to-signal progression

Track material through these states:

`source -> source-cluster -> recurring-signal -> reusable-mechanism -> primitive-candidate -> tested-primitive -> adopted-capability`

Use stable identifiers and cross-links. Multiple sources may support one mechanism; do not duplicate the mechanism merely because the sources differ.

## Retention model

Retain a source inside Singularity Science when it has potential value but cannot yet support a generic primitive, validated decision, or approved receiving-lane transfer because of uncertainty, provenance, rights, sensitivity, contradiction, or insufficient recurrence.

Use these retention statuses:

- `watch`: plausible signal with a defined recurrence or corroboration trigger.
- `needs-verification`: consequential claim blocked by missing evidence.
- `worldview-only`: useful for understanding framing or rhetoric, but not for operational inference.

Every retained source must record:

```text
Retention reason:
Current status:
Evidence missing:
Revisit trigger:
Review owner:
Next review date:
Potential receiving lane:
```

Retention is a valid protective outcome. Promotion requires corroboration, clarified boundaries, a reusable abstraction, or human-approved downstream use. Expired and disproven signals are recorded rather than silently discarded.

## Decision Compression and value receipts

Every Standard or Deep analysis begins with:

```text
What changed:
Reusable mechanism:
Decision implication:
Evidence still missing:
Recommended disposition:
```

Every material receipt should also capture:

```text
Research question:
Decision or risk affected:
Baseline assumption:
Human authority required:
Downstream receipt reference:
Outcome after review:
Review date:
Counterfactual:
Attribution confidence:
```

The strongest proof of value is a validated reusable primitive per unit of operator effort. Also record changed decisions, earlier detection, avoided errors, justified non-action, and improved monitoring.

Do not use transcript count, word count, citation count, or candidate count as standalone value measures.

## Prioritization and feedback

Prioritize work using expected decision relevance, novelty, recurrence, source independence, governance sensitivity, likely transfer value, and processing cost.

Every routed insight receives one downstream outcome:

`accepted | modified | rejected | held | adopted | caused-unintended-work`

Feed outcomes back into source-type, analyst, lane, and mechanism reviews.

## Lane rules

Innermost Loop uses one research-ledger row per source or source cluster and emphasizes product implication, governance pressure, primitive potential, authority-leak risk, recurrence, and retention status.

Moonshots, Nate B. Jones, Nate Herk, and External Interviews use seam-first extraction with 2-5 seams and exactly one ROI disposition per seam:

`primitive-candidate | needs-verification | lane-test-ready | worldview-only | preserved`

Every `lane-test-ready` seam must include:

```text
Source episode or Source interview:
Seam:
Transferable question or checklist:
Receiving lane:
Membrane classification:
Human authority required:
Evidence still needed:
What stays inside Singularity Science:
```

Preserve rhetoric, promotion, benchmarks, timelines, and source-specific claims as source-attributed and verification-sensitive.

## Stop boundary

This skill does not automatically preserve a primitive, run a formal reality check, perform recurrence review, publish an implications memo, or implement a receiving-lane change. It prepares the governed receipt and hands off to the appropriate skill or human authority.
