# Executive Council–Singularity Science Interface Contract

**Version:** `0.3`

**Status:** `proposed — no authority or standing mandate created`

**Scope:** Anyang Intelligence internal operations

**Decision owner:** System Engineer

## Purpose

Singularity Science is Anyang Intelligence's governed research and foresight
lane. It converts already-available source material into attributable signals,
evidence gaps, recurrence judgments, and candidate operating mechanisms.

Executive Council may use those outputs when preparing integrated judgment.
Neither surface replaces the other:

- Singularity Science investigates.
- Executive Council integrates and recommends.
- The System Engineer decides what may proceed.
- Applicable client owners, property owners, parents, and guardians retain
  their own authority.

This proposed contract creates no Council role, runtime, authority,
persistence, publication, customer action, commercial offer, or standing
mandate.

## Structural relationship

Singularity Science:

- is part of the Anyang Intelligence project portfolio;
- is not a member, committee, or subordinate agent of Executive Council;
- holds no independent executive, customer, publication, spending, or
  capability-adoption authority;
- may receive bounded research questions through the tasking contract below;
  and
- returns research evidence rather than decisions disguised as research.

Executive Council:

- has a shared read surface across repository-visible Singularity Science
  artifacts, subject to applicable membranes;
- may use attributed synthesis in internal judgment;
- does not acquire rights to republish sources or move transcript bulk; and
- may not treat a source claim, repeated claim, or primitive candidate as
  adopted doctrine.

The [Executive Council role
contract](executive-council-role-contract.md) remains controlling for Council
roles, decision classes, tasking, and authority. The [Singularity Science
project charter](../projects/singularity-science/README.md) remains controlling
for the research lane's purpose, archive, outputs, and boundary.

## Tasking contract

The normal tasker of Singularity Science is the Chief Executive, operating
inside an approved objective and a System Engineer-defined authority envelope.

The governing distinction is:

```text
request a question != task the lane != approve execution != adopt the result
```

| Function | Authority |
| --- | --- |
| Propose a research question | Any Council role or project owner |
| Frame and prioritize the research task | Chief Executive |
| Approve or change a standing mandate | System Engineer or explicitly delegated authority acting within a current receipt |
| Approve work outside a standing mandate | System Engineer or explicitly delegated authority acting within a current receipt |
| Perform the research | Executor named in the current approval or standing mandate, explicitly operating through the governed Singularity Science skill |
| Audit provenance and claimed state | Independently activated Council Steward |
| Approve cross-project use or adoption | System Engineer plus the applicable project, client, property, parent, or guardian authority |
| Communicate externally | Executive Assistant under an approved dispatch |

### Class 0 — read-only research

The Chief Executive may task read-only Singularity Science research only when:

- the objective is already approved;
- source material is already available and permitted;
- work remains read-only and internal;
- no private access, external contact, spending, publication, or persistence
  occurs; and
- the result is advisory and returns to Executive Council.

### Class 1 — durable internal research

Creating or modifying transcripts, source notes, analyses, ledgers, recurrence
packets, or retained research state requires either:

- exact System Engineer approval for the bounded task; or
- a written standing mandate that identifies the task class, named executor,
  permitted lanes, source classes, files, schemas, limits, required evidence,
  validation, term and expiry, review date, stop conditions, revocation path,
  and recovery method.

This proposed interface contract is not itself a standing mandate. No general
standing mandate should be inferred from prior research activity, repository
access, repeated tasking, or the existence of the Singularity Science skill.

### Protected or external work

Separate authority is always required for:

- new private-source access;
- paid research, subscriptions, or procurement;
- external interviews, stakeholder contact, or source requests;
- publication or public claims;
- customer- or project-specific implementation;
- primitive testing or capability adoption;
- rights clearance;
- legal, financial, tax, medical, educational, child-safety, or property
  conclusions; and
- cross-project transfer of anything beyond an approved abstraction.

The Executive Assistant may relay an approved request but does not become the
research tasker. The Artistic Director may propose research needs but routes
them through the Chief Executive. The Council Steward audits research state
and receipts; it does not direct the research agenda or approve its findings.

### Controlling tasking clause

> The Chief Executive is the normal tasker of Singularity Science within an
> approved objective and, for Class 1 work, under an exact approval or current
> standing mandate. Any Council role or project owner may propose a research
> question, but a request does not become an authorized task until the Chief
> Executive frames its decision purpose, source boundary, expected output, and
> decision owner. The System Engineer or explicitly delegated authority acting
> within a current receipt may establish or expand a standing mandate and must
> separately approve durable research, new access, testing, cross-project
> transfer, publication, spending, or capability adoption. Delegation applies
> only to the authority, scope, term, and executor named in its controlling
> receipt.

The canonical source-to-signal progression is:

```text
source
  -> source-cluster
  -> recurring-signal
  -> reusable-mechanism
  -> primitive-candidate
  -> tested-primitive
  -> adopted-capability
```

Research may propose progression only through `primitive-candidate`.
Authorization to test is a separate authority gate, not a progression state.
`tested-primitive` requires an approved test, a named executor, and returned
test evidence. `adopted-capability` requires separate adoption authority and
evidence. A later artifact may refine an earlier judgment without silently
overwriting the earlier artifact's state.

## Decision and authority rules

Every state-bearing handoff must name its state namespace and controlling
artifact. The namespaces are not interchangeable:

- **source-progression state:** `source | source-cluster | recurring-signal |
  reusable-mechanism | primitive-candidate | tested-primitive |
  adopted-capability`;
- **retention status:** `watch | needs-verification | worldview-only`;
- **lane ROI disposition:** `primitive-candidate | needs-verification |
  lane-test-ready | worldview-only | preserved`;
- **recurrence disposition:** `watch | verify | preserve-candidate |
  lane-test-ready | drop`.

Use `not applicable` when a namespace does not govern the artifact and
`Missing` when required evidence or state is absent. `Hold` is an interface
control response, not a substitute for any Singularity Science disposition.
None of these labels authorizes testing, installation, publication, spending,
customer action, policy change, or capability adoption.

A Council recommendation is not approval. System Engineer approval does not
create client-company, property-owner, parent, or guardian authority. Client,
property-owner, parent, or guardian approval does not create Anyang
Intelligence system authority.

## Membrane contract

The following may cross into Executive Council:

- original synthesis;
- source identifiers and citations;
- evidence gaps and contradictions;
- abstract mechanisms;
- lane-safe questions; and
- rights and uncertainty warnings.

The following remain inside Singularity Science unless separately reviewed:

- transcript bulk and long quotations;
- promotional rhetoric;
- unverified benchmarks, incidents, forecasts, prices, and product claims;
- source-specific instructions or copyrighted material;
- private, sensitive, or customer-local context; and
- contact information and credentials.

Cross-project transfer requires a reusable abstraction, named receiving lane,
membrane review, and applicable human approval. The [repository membrane
contract](membranes.md) remains controlling.

## Hold conditions

Return `Hold` when:

- source identity, provenance, rights, or lane assignment is ambiguous;
- a request requires unavailable private evidence or new access;
- independent recurrence is confused with repeated commentary;
- the proposed handoff could alter permissions, authority, safety, privacy, or
  customer state;
- a receiving lane or decision owner is missing;
- the finding depends on unstable facts that have not been verified; or
- external publication, contact, spending, installation, or testing is implied
  without approval.

## Feedback loop

Every routed finding should eventually receive one outcome:

```text
accepted
modified
rejected
held
adopted
caused-unintended-work
```

The outcome should return to Singularity Science with the decision owner,
evidence, review date, and next revisit condition. Selection frequency,
rhetorical appeal, or repeated citation is not outcome evidence.

## Cadence

- Research remains event-driven within its established lane cadence.
- Executive Council receives only decision-relevant synthesis, not routine
  archive volume.
- A quarterly review should examine stale signals, unresolved verification
  queues, adopted mechanisms, and review burden.
- Authority, privacy, safety, or membrane incidents surface immediately.

## Commercial and public boundary

Singularity Science is not presently a separate company, customer service,
certification body, or public research institute.

Any future external product requires separate approval of:

- name and trademark position;
- audience and offer;
- source and publication rights;
- methodology and evidence claims;
- pricing and commercial authority;
- customer-data boundary; and
- separation between internal research and public deliverables.

## Governing sentence

> Singularity Science supplies attributable research intelligence to
> Executive Council; Executive Council prepares integrated judgment from that
> intelligence; the appropriate human authority decides what may become
> action.

## Interface acceptance case

Use this case to test state lineage before adoption:

> The Artistic Director proposes whether Nate Herk's tool-agnostic context seam
> should inform infrastructure planning for a Codex-and-Cursor workflow. The
> Chief Executive frames a read-only question using already-available sources.
> The source analysis assigns the seam a lane ROI disposition of
> `lane-test-ready`; a later recurrence review assigns it a recurrence
> disposition of `watch` because independent evidence remains insufficient.

The interface passes only when the returned handoff:

- reports both dispositions with their namespaces and controlling artifacts;
- preserves the later recurrence judgment without rewriting the earlier
  analysis;
- routes only the translated portability question across the membrane;
- keeps transcript bulk, source-specific preferences, and universal tool claims
  inside Singularity Science;
- states that no infrastructure change, tool choice, persistence, or test has
  been authorized; and
- names the required approver, executor, evidence, expiry, revocation path, and
  recovery method before any Class 1 test can begin.

## Pressure-test evidence

Both tests below were conducted as read-only review exercises on 2026-08-05.
They created no research state, standing mandate, receiving-lane artifact,
runtime activation, implementation authority, or external action.

### Test 1 — Nate Herk state lineage

**Question:** Could the Nate Herk tool-agnostic context seam inform
infrastructure planning for a Codex-and-Cursor workflow?

**Evidence reviewed:** [source analysis](../projects/singularity-science/archive/nate-herk/analyses/2026-08-04-everything-ive-learned-after-5000-hours-of-building-with-ai.analysis.md),
[recurrence review](../projects/singularity-science/archive/recurrence-reviews/2026-08-04-nate-herk-evidence-before-authority.md),
and [ROI ledger](../projects/singularity-science/archive/nate-herk/roi-ledger.md).

**Result:** `pass after state-model correction`. The interface preserves the
analysis-level `lane-test-ready` ROI disposition and the later recurrence-level
`watch` disposition as separate, simultaneously valid judgments. Neither label
authorizes an infrastructure change, tool choice, persistence, or test.

### Test 2 — Mountain Villa cross-project membrane

**Question:** Could the ambient-agency review gate inform a Mountain Villa
seasonal-monitoring workflow?

**Evidence reviewed:** [ambient-agency review gate](../projects/singularity-science/primitives/ambient-agency-review-gate.md),
[Mountain Villa membrane](../projects/mountain-villa/membrane-notes.md), and the
[repository membrane contract](membranes.md).

**Result:** `pass with Hold`. Only the generic question of whether monitoring
surfaces signals or silently substitutes for owner risk judgment may cross.
Property facts, access details, vulnerabilities, security information,
insurance context, vendor claims, and emergency instructions remain local.
Property-owner approval and separate Anyang Intelligence authority remain
required before any receiving-lane use or test.

## Adoption gate

Before this contract may move from `proposed` to `adopted`, the System Engineer
should review:

- consistency with the Executive Council role contract and current authority
  envelope;
- whether a separate standing mandate is needed;
- at least one real handoff pressure test with durable evidence;
- Council Steward review of state and membrane language;
- validation of local links and controlling references; and
- the exact effective date, review date, and supersession rule.
