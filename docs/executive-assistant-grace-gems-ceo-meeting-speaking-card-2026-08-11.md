# Executive Assistant Speaking Card — Grace Gems CEO Meeting

**Purpose:** Obtain four bounded CEO decisions. **Duration:** 60 minutes.

**Boundary:** This card authorizes no invitation, outreach, production,
spending, account creation, deployment, DNS change, publication, or
implementation.

## Open — 5 minutes

> Today we will confirm the Grace Mar–Grace Gems direction, review the proposed
> Artistic Director work, map minimum communication infrastructure, and correct
> the cost-and-revenue picture. What in our current record is inaccurate or
> incomplete?

Record each correction with speaker, capacity, and evidence source.

**Current direction:** Grace Mar is the intended commercial umbrella; Grace
Gems is the intended flagship jewelry brand. Legal ownership, asset control,
organizational containment, and public wording remain unresolved.

> Which relationship may the public site state: developing association,
> planned flagship, current flagship, or hold?
>

> control, or organizational containment?

> flagship jewelry direction.

Capture: exact wording; Grace Mar and Grace Gems approving capacities; `Yes /
No / Unresolved` for ownership, asset control, and containment; qualifier;
evidence; review date.

## 2. Artistic Director cohort — 15 minutes

**Current state:** JK holds the role. AI runtime and first task remain inactive.
Client-specific production is held because no private engagement surface is
approved.

> Should the three-territory rebranding cohort `Proceed / Revise / Hold /
> Reject`?

Ask and capture:

- What CEO decision should the final PDF support?
- What evidence may JK and AI use?
- What must not be assumed about customers, products, sourcing, history, or
  strategy?

and task authority; Executive Assistant relays approved intent and evidence.

or deployment; domain, DNS, or email changes; accounts, posts, ads, or customer
