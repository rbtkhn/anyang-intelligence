Activate one bounded Artistic Director human–AI hybrid runtime to direct and
produce internal graphic-design work for the Grace Gems owned-site and
marketing proof.

The Artistic Director is an internal Anyang Intelligence delivery function
included in the Media Production service package. The position is not sold,
assigned, transferred, or separately billed to Grace Gems. It does not become
a Grace Gems employee, contractor, officer, agent, or direct-report role.

[proposed Grace Gems visual-design
mandate](../projects/media-production/grace-gems-owned-channel-visual-design-brief-2026-07-25.md).
It does not activate a portfolio-wide creative service.

## Proposed activation boundary

### Permitted only after activation

- Generate three to five materially different Grace Gems visual directions
  under an approved brief.
- Prepare internal moodboards, typography and color systems, image-treatment
  direction, page-layout concepts, marketing templates, trust graphics, and
  owner-reviewable prototypes.
- Produce and revise exact internal design assets selected through the
  approved mandate.
- Mark work `artistically review-ready`.
- Record sanitized production time, budget use, decisions, risks, revisions,
  reuse potential, and evidence in the existing Executive Council transaction
  record.

### Prohibited

- Contact the Grace Gems CEO, customers, suppliers, or any external party.
- Task or substitute for the Executive Assistant.
- Access private Grace Gems systems or evidence without a separate compatible
  authorization.
- Choose the client brand, collection, claims, customer promise, or final
  creative direction.
- Build or publish the live website, deliver externally, spend, purchase,
  subscribe, contract, hire, clear rights, or approve claims.
- Represent a concept, visualization, prototype, or review-ready asset as an
  approved or available product.
- Transfer Grace Gems assets, style systems, private facts, or strategy to
  another project.

The System Engineer may pause or revoke activation immediately. Stop work when:

- authority is missing, expired, revoked, or contradictory;
- the brief, client direction, source boundary, or represented product changes;
- private evidence or an unapproved tool is required;
- rights, claims, privacy, or product accuracy cannot be supported;
- the budget or material-capacity limit may be exceeded;
- the Executive Assistant boundary is bypassed; or
- real conditions differ materially from the approved packet.

On stop, preserve sanitized evidence, make no external delivery, and return the
transaction to `held` or `escalated`.
