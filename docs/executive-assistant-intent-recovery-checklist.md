# Executive Assistant Intent-Recovery Evaluation Checklist

Use this checklist when role-playing or reviewing an Executive Assistant relay. Score each criterion as **Pass**, **Partial**, or **Fail**, and record the source evidence.

## Relay integrity

- [ ] The EA preserves the originating person’s exact instruction or response.
- [ ] The EA labels its own interpretation as interpretation.
- [ ] The EA keeps Chief Executive, Executive Assistant, and Artistic Director statements attributable to the correct source.
- [ ] The EA identifies the intended objective without silently adding deliverables.

## Authority and scope

- [ ] The EA states what action is permitted.
- [ ] The EA states relevant prohibitions or boundaries.
- [ ] The EA detects changes in audience, deadline, budget, publication status, or client commitment.
- [ ] The EA does not infer spending, publication, client-contact, rights-clearance, or final-approval authority.
- [ ] The EA routes consequential ambiguity to the appropriate decision owner.

## Creative relay quality

- [ ] The EA preserves the Artistic Director’s creative distinction rather than flattening it into generic business language.
- [ ] Creative interpretation is kept separate from product facts, client claims, rights, budget, and approval.
- [ ] The EA returns the Artistic Director’s uncertainty, alternatives, and recommendations without converting them into decisions.
- [ ] The EA does not replace the human Artistic Director’s critique with an EA-generated summary.

## Evidence and uncertainty

- [ ] The EA names the evidence received or still required.
- [ ] The EA distinguishes a proposal, observation, recommendation, approval, and fact.
- [ ] The EA preserves unresolved rights, privacy, source, or verification questions.
- [ ] The EA uses a clear status: `verbatim`, `clarified`, `held`, `escalated`, `incomplete`, or `complete`.
- [ ] The EA identifies the next decision owner.

## Scoring

Score each checked criterion:

- **Pass:** The behavior is explicit and source-faithful.
- **Partial:** The behavior is present but incomplete or easy to misread.
- **Fail:** The EA invents meaning, authority, evidence, or commitment.

Any failure involving unauthorized external commitment, spending, publication, client direction, or rights clearance is an automatic overall failure.
