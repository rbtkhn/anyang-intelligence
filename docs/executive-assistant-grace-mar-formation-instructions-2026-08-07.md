## Purpose

Prepare and, only after an exact approved dispatch, coordinate the formation
and initial operating infrastructure of Grace Mar.

The approved direction is:

```text
Grace Mar LLC
└── Grace Mar — bounded commercial umbrella

    └── Future related ventures admitted by explicit decision
```

Grace Mar is not the default owner or brand for Anyang Intelligence, Media
Production, Learning Core, Mountain Villa, Book Club, or another portfolio
project.

## Phase 1 — return a decision-ready preparation packet

Perform only read-only preparation until an authority disposition approves
specific execution.

### A. LLC formation inputs

Return the following as `Confirmed`, `Proposed`, or `Missing`:

- formation jurisdiction;
- exact legal name and official name-availability result;
- organizer;
- member or members and ownership percentages;
- each member's contribution and agreed valuation;
- member-managed or manager-managed structure;
- registered agent and consent;

- effective date;
- operating-agreement owner and review path;
- approved filing channel;

- tax and accounting reviewer;

Do not place personal addresses, identification numbers, payment information,
credentials, signatures, or raw formation records in Git.

If Colorado is selected, use the official Colorado Secretary of State sources,
including the [LLC filing
checklist](https://www.sos.state.co.us/pubs/business/helpFiles/LLCChecklist.pdf)
and [business record
search](https://sos.state.co.us/biz/FileDocSearchCriteria.do?quitButtonDestination=BusinessFunctions).
Do not infer Colorado merely from existing project locations.

### C. Domain and Google Workspace inputs

Confirm without exposing credentials:

- domain registrar;
- operator control of the registrar account;
- administrative and technical contacts;
- expiration and auto-renewal state;
- approved payment owner;
- recovery method and second accountable administrator;
- current DNS state;
- selected Google Workspace edition and current quoted cost;
- primary Workspace administrator;
- initial users, groups, and aliases;
- proposed email-security records; and
- evidence and recovery location outside Git.

Propose the smallest useful initial address set on the approved
`grace-mar.com` domain:

- one named individual account for each approved user;
- `hello` local part;
- `support` local part;
- `social` local part;
- `legal` local part; and
- `finance` local part.

Identify which addresses should be licensed users, groups, or aliases before
purchase. Do not create unnecessary paid seats.

Google requires control and verification of the domain for custom-domain
Workspace email. Its setup uses DNS and MX records; SPF and DKIM should be
included in the security plan. See [Google Workspace domain
setup](https://support.google.com/a/answer/7009324) and [Google DNS
guidance](https://support.google.com/a/answer/48090).

## Phase 2 — execution after approval

If and only if the approved dispatch covers the applicable action, execute in
this order:

1. Recheck legal-name availability and filing facts immediately before filing.
2. File the LLC through the approved official government channel.
3. Return the filing confirmation and expense evidence to the approved private
   location.
4. Do not represent the LLC as active until the official filing state supports
   that claim.
5. Record the LLC as the intended owner or licensee of approved brand assets;
   complete transfers only when separately authorized and documented.
6. Verify `grace-mar.com` in Google Workspace and configure only the approved
   users, groups, aliases, MX records, and email-security records.
7. Test inbound and outbound email without sending marketing or customer
   communication.
8. Create only the specifically approved social accounts, using the approved
   domain address, accountable human owner, recovery owner, and two-factor
   method.
9. Do not post, advertise, invite contacts, message customers, or change an
   existing Grace Gems account unless separately approved.
10. Return one action receipt per legal filing, paid subscription, domain or
    email change, and social-platform account.

Government, tax, banking, payment, and platform actions may require additional
approval, private information, or professional review. Stop rather than
extending the dispatch by inference.

## Stop and escalate

Stop without executing or continuing when:

- jurisdiction, ownership, contribution value, management, or registered-agent
  authority is missing or contradictory;
- the legal name or a material brand name is unavailable or contested;
- a filing, subscription, or purchase exceeds its approved ceiling;
- a system requests private information outside the approved handling path;
- registrar control or domain recovery is uncertain;
- a trademark, rights, testimonial, customer-data, or existing-account issue
  appears;
- a platform requires a new public claim, verification statement, or contact;
- existing Grace Gems assets would need transfer, renaming, migration, or
  deletion;
- another person requests equity or ownership;
- the work creates an unexpected contract, recurring charge, or commitment; or
- the intended public brand, legal entity, or owner changes.

Return the exact blocker, consequence, evidence available, missing decision,
and next decision owner.
