## Recommendation

Develop this as a higher-value extension of the AI Repository Assurance Review,
not as an ordinary standalone website audit.

The integrated offer tests two connected questions:

1. Does the website work clearly and reliably for customers and search
   systems?
2. Can its important content, claims, and operating instructions be traced to
   current, supportable repository state?

The commercial distinction is recurrence prevention. A conventional website
audit may find a slow page, missing alternative text, duplicate title, or
confusing shipping promise. The integrated Anyang review also asks why the
problem exists, which upstream source controls it, whether AI will reproduce
it, and what decision would prevent it from returning.

No live website, Grace Gems system, analytics account, private repository, or
client evidence is authorized for review by this design.

## Simple customer promise

> **We check what customers see on your website and what your AI uses behind
> it, then show which few fixes will improve the site without letting the same
> mistakes return.**

The customer buys one connected decision brief rather than a website score and
a repository report that must be reconciled manually.

## Illustrative jewelry-business example

### Before

- A product page says an item ships from the United States.
- A policy page implies overseas fulfillment.
- The AI content workspace contains both statements and no controlling
  approval.
- Product imagery is slow on mobile.
- Alternative text repeats generic product titles.
- An old supplier timeline remains in the page-generation instructions.

### What the integrated review finds

- **Website symptom:** contradictory fulfillment language can confuse buyers.
- **Repository cause:** two current-looking source files disagree and neither
  contains an owner approval.
- **Website symptom:** slow product-page loading weakens mobile experience.
- **Repository cause:** the asset-production brief has no image-size or
  delivery-format constraint.
- **Website symptom:** generic alternative text weakens accessibility and
  product understanding.
- **Repository cause:** the generation template treats alternative text as an
  SEO field rather than a description of the image's function and content.

### After the review

The owner receives a short sequence:

1. decide the authoritative fulfillment rule;
2. correct the upstream product and policy source;
3. regenerate only the affected pages;
4. add image-size and alternative-text controls to the production brief;
5. retest the website;
6. preserve the evidence and owner approval that now control future AI output.

The review does not make the owner decisions, change the website, or represent
that conversion will improve.

## Best-fit customer

- Founder-led or small team, usually 1–50 people.
- One public or staging website and one repository materially shape the same
  customer experience.
- AI generates or maintains website copy, product information, policy text,
  images, structured content, or implementation instructions.
- The owner is experiencing inconsistent claims, repeated content errors,
  unclear source ownership, poor mobile quality, a migration, or a website
  redesign.
- The owner can authorize both the website surface and the exact repository
  snapshot.
- At least one critical customer journey or business decision is named before
  review.

Strong initial segments:

- ecommerce brands;
- AI-assisted editorial or research publishers;
- professional-service firms with generated knowledge content;
- small software companies whose documentation site and operating repository
  share state; and
- agencies maintaining AI-assisted client sites under explicit client
  authority.

## Founding-pilot scope

### Website surface

- One public website or explicitly authorized staging deployment.
- Up to 25 unique URLs.
- Up to five representative page types, such as homepage, collection,
  product, policy, and contact or checkout-entry.
- Mobile and desktop automated lab runs for the representative pages.
- Status, redirect, canonical, metadata, heading, image, link, and basic
  structured-data inspection.
- Crawl and indexability review using publicly observable signals.
- Automated accessibility indicators plus manual sampling of three critical
  tasks.
- Up to three customer journeys without purchase, submission, account
  creation, or consequential transaction.
- Up to 20 customer-facing claims traced toward their controlling repository
  source and approval state.
- Visual clarity and consistency observations only when an Artistic Director
  runtime is separately active and exactly tasked.

### Repository surface

- One Git repository.
- One sealed branch, commit, or approved dirty-snapshot manifest.
- Up to 5,000 tracked files and 250 MB of tracked content.
- Up to two repository-declared read-only native commands.
- Full tracked-file inventory and fingerprint.
- Structured-file, link, machine-path, and declared-control diagnostics.
- Deterministic review of up to 20 repository trace chains, including the
  website claims selected above.
- Root-cause consolidation and mutation proof.

### Excluded

- Website or repository mutation.
- Checkout, purchase, form submission, customer contact, account creation, or
  destructive interaction.
- Penetration testing, vulnerability assurance, credential testing, payment
  security, PCI assessment, privacy-law opinion, cookie-consent certification,
  or legal review.
- WCAG conformance certification or complete assistive-technology testing.
- Search-ranking guarantee, backlink analysis, competitive keyword campaign,
  or paid-media review.
- Conversion-rate claim without approved analytics and a valid baseline.
- Private analytics, customer, order, payment, supplier, margin, or session
  evidence unless separately authorized under an approved membrane.
- Remediation, redesign, copy replacement, publication, or deployment.
- Domain-fact verification beyond the approved evidence surface.

Unexpected scope returns to qualification. It never silently enlarges the
fixed fee or evidence surface.

## Website review standard

The website layer uses current authoritative guidance as a reference, not as a
claim of certification:

- [Chrome Lighthouse](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/lighthouse/overview)
  supplies automated indicators for performance, accessibility, best
  practices, and SEO;
- [Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/defining-core-web-vitals-thresholds)
  define current experience measures for loading, responsiveness, and visual
  stability;
- [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/new-in-22/)
  informs the sampled accessibility review; and
- the [Google SEO Starter
  Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide)
  informs crawlability, content understanding, titles, descriptions, URLs,
  canonicalization, and image context.

Automated lab results vary with environment and do not substitute for field
data. A Lighthouse accessibility score is not proof of WCAG conformance.
Search guidance does not guarantee indexing, ranking, traffic, or revenue.

When field Core Web Vitals are publicly available, report lab and field
measurements separately. When they are unavailable, say so rather than
representing lab measurements as real-user experience.

## Integrated finding model

Every material finding uses one row:

| Field | Required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Customer-visible symptom | What a user, search system, or operator encounters |
| Website evidence | Exact URL, page type, test context, and observation |
| Repository dependency | Controlling or candidate source path and state |
| Claim or control status | Supported, contradicted, stale, inaccessible, or environment-only |
| Root cause | Smallest supported upstream cause |
| Consequence | Trust, accessibility, discovery, performance, conversion risk, or operating burden |
| Required authority | Website owner, client CEO, System Engineer, or specialist review |
| Recommended disposition | `retain / fix / narrow / retire / investigate` |
| Retest | Exact evidence needed to show the issue changed |

Do not force a repository cause when none is evidenced. Some website failures
are deployment, platform, network, or third-party-service problems. Mark them
as website-only rather than inventing lineage.

## Deliverables

Use three consolidated customer-facing artifacts:

1. **Sealed scope and machine receipt**
   - website URLs, page types, timestamp, tool versions, device profiles, and
     access boundary;
   - repository identity, commands, inventory, and mutation proof;
   - raw diagnostics and coverage gaps.
2. **Independent integrated finding ledger**
   - website-only defects;
   - repository-only defects;
   - website-to-repository trace findings;
   - supported controls;
   - inaccessible or private evidence;
   - consolidated root causes;
   - sealed Council Steward findings.
3. **Owner decision brief**
   - top three to five decisions;
   - customer-impact and recurrence-prevention priority;
   - immediate holds;
   - bounded remediation options;
   - retest requirements;
   - what the evidence does not establish.

The customer does not receive raw internal Council deliberation, unrelated
portfolio state, private runtime details, or another client's examples.

## Delivery and authority cycle

```text
Customer names the website, repository, and critical journey
  -> Executive Assistant gathers bounded qualification evidence
  -> Chief Executive prepares scope, price, exclusions, and decision objective
  -> System Engineer authorizes Anyang access and execution
  -> website/repository owner authorizes both customer surfaces
  -> named executor seals repository and website test context
  -> mechanical collectors run read-only
  -> Council Steward independently reviews primary evidence and trace chains
  -> Artistic Director reviews visual clarity only if separately activated
  -> Chief Executive ranks business consequences and recurrence prevention
  -> System Engineer approves the external packet
  -> Executive Assistant delivers and records receipt
  -> remediation or retesting requires a new authorization
```

Client-company authority and Anyang authority remain parallel. Public website
availability does not by itself authorize commercial review, private data
access, representation, or client action.

## Role boundaries

### Chief Executive

- Defines the commercial and decision objective.
- Chooses the bounded review strategy.
- Interprets operating and customer consequences.
- Does not approve claims, price the live engagement without System Engineer
  approval, access client-private systems, or deliver externally.

### Council Steward

- Reviews sealed website and repository evidence independently.
- Tests whether cross-surface lineage and state claims are supported.
- Seals findings before Chief Executive interpretation.
- Does not perform design, strategy, sales, remediation, or approval.

### Artistic Director

- May review hierarchy, visual coherence, product representation, mobile
  legibility, and trust presentation under an approved brief.
- Does not certify accessibility, approve client direction, rewrite findings,
  publish, or contact the client.
- Is omitted when no Artistic Director runtime is active; no empty receipt is
  required.

### Executive Assistant

- Qualifies and communicates only under an approved dispatch.
- Handles permitted client coordination and returns evidence.
- Does not infer website, repository, analytics, or client-company authority
  from access or prior contact.

### System Engineer and website owner

- System Engineer approves Anyang scope, tools, price, access, and delivery.
- The website or client owner approves access and all client-company
  decisions.
- Neither authority substitutes for the other.

## Proof-before-sale gate

Do not sell the integrated offer until the parent repository kernel clears its
proof gate and:

- one owner-controlled website/repository pair completes the integrated review;
- every website test records URL, time, tool version, device profile, and
  access state;
- at least 80% of material integrated findings are accepted without
  substantive revision;
- at least 70% of accepted website findings either trace to a supported
  upstream cause or are honestly classified as website-only;
- repository and website collection each reach at least 90% of declared scope;
- a second run against the same unchanged surfaces produces materially
  consistent results;
- the full review fits within 12 Anyang hours;
- automated accessibility findings are manually qualified and never called
  conformance;
- lab and field performance evidence remain distinct;
- no search-ranking, conversion, compliance, or security guarantee appears;
- customer-safe redaction and deletion controls pass;
- there are zero unauthorized writes, transactions, private-data events,
  protected-context transfers, or external communications.

Grace Gems must not become this proof pair until the Grace Gems CEO and System
Engineer separately authorize the exact website, repository, public/private
evidence, analytics boundary, reviewers, retention, and action exclusions.
Existing website plans do not supply that approval.

## Commercial scorecard

### Website quality

- URLs and page types successfully reviewed;
- broken journeys and links;
- mobile and desktop lab indicators;
- field Core Web Vitals availability and state;
- manually qualified accessibility findings;
- crawl/index and metadata defects;
- claim contradictions;
- visual clarity and trust holds;
- website-only versus upstream-linked findings.

### Recurrence prevention

- percentage of accepted website findings with supported upstream causes;
- number of controlling causes after symptom consolidation;
- affected pages per root cause;
- accepted controls added during later remediation;
- retest pass rate;
- recurrence at 30 and 90 days.

### Economics and customer value

- qualification, collection, review, delivery, and reconciliation minutes;
- direct cost, realized price, contribution, and margin;
- time for the owner to identify the first three decisions;
- owner-rated decision usefulness;
- remediation and retest conversion;
- repeat-review conversion;
- customer-reported rework avoided, clearly labeled when estimated.

Conversion, revenue improvement, and avoided-loss claims remain unsupported
until the customer supplies an approved baseline and subsequent evidence.

## Risks and controls

| Risk | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Two audits create a larger unprioritized report | One integrated root-cause ledger and three-to-five-decision brief |
| Lighthouse score becomes the product | Treat automated results as evidence inputs, not conclusions |
| Accessibility review implies legal conformity | Explicit sampling boundary; no certification or legal claim |
| SEO advice implies ranking | Report crawl and content conditions; guarantee no ranking or traffic result |
| Website symptom is forced into a repository narrative | Permit website-only and inaccessible classifications |
| Public access is mistaken for client authority | Require named website/repository owner authorization |
| AI-generated remediation spreads unsupported claims | Review ends before mutation; remediation requires a new gate |
| Artistic review changes evidence severity | Keep Artistic Director observations separately attributable |
| Private analytics expands fixed scope | Exclude by default; require a separate membrane and price |
