## The offer

> **Anyang Intelligence tests whether an AI-operated repository's declared
> state, controls, and evidence lineage are trustworthy enough to rely on—and
> reduces noisy symptoms to the few decisions that matter.**

AI-assisted repositories often look healthy while their instructions,
manifests, generated outputs, approval states, or publication paths disagree.
Ordinary tests may pass because they check individual components rather than
the operating claims the repository asks its owner to trust.

The founding review is a sealed, read-only assessment of one repository. It
does not change the repository.

## Best fit

The review is designed for a founder, technical operator, research director,
editorial director, or AI operations lead when:

- a repository is an operating environment or knowledge system, not merely
  source-code storage;
- AI agents create or maintain important artifacts;
- a migration, launch, customer delivery, or reusable template depends on the
  repository being coherent;
- native validation is noisy, incomplete, or reassuring without answering
  whether the declared workflow actually works;
- the owner needs a small number of evidence-backed decisions rather than a
  long defect list.

## Founding-pilot boundary

Included:

- one Git repository;
- one sealed branch, commit, or approved dirty-snapshot manifest;
- up to 5,000 tracked files and 250 MB of tracked content;
- up to two repository-declared read-only commands;
- full tracked-file inventory and snapshot fingerprint;
- structured-file, link, machine-local-path, and declared-control checks;
- up to 20 deterministic high-consequence trace chains;
- independent assurance review;
- root-cause consolidation;
- one executive decision brief;
- one separately approved 60-minute readout;
- one bounded remediation-plan option.

Excluded:

- repository changes or cleanup;
- issues, pull requests, commits, pushes, publication, or deployment;
- penetration testing or cybersecurity assurance;
- dependency-vulnerability or secret-scanning guarantees;
- legal, financial, privacy, accessibility, or compliance certification;
- external fact-checking of the repository's subject matter;
- production-system or private-data access outside an exact approved boundary;
- more than one reconciliation round.

Unexpected scope returns to qualification. It does not silently expand the
fixed fee.

## What the customer receives

1. **Snapshot and collector receipt**
   - exact repository identity and reviewed state;
   - commands, results, duration, inventory, diagnostics, and coverage gaps;
   - evidence that the target was not changed.
2. **Independent assurance ledger**
   - supported controls;
   - verified defects and contradictions;
   - inferred risks and inaccessible evidence;
   - environment-only failures;
   - consolidated root causes.
3. **Executive decision brief**
   - highest-consequence decisions;
   - `retain / fix / narrow / retire / investigate` recommendations;
   - immediate holds;
   - remediation boundaries and required evidence.

The customer receives decision-useful findings, not raw internal Council
deliberation or unrelated portfolio context.

## Customer inputs required

Before work begins, the customer must provide or approve:

- proof of repository authority;
- the exact repository and snapshot boundary;
- one consequential decision the review should support;
- permitted read-only commands and timeouts;
- controlling files or directories;
- excluded evidence and systems;
- data classification, retention, and deletion requirements;
- one technical contact for factual clarification;
- authority for Anyang Intelligence to perform and deliver the review.

Anyang Intelligence must separately approve scope, access, price, terms,
execution, and external delivery. Customer authorization and Anyang authority
do not substitute for each other.

## Proposed delivery experience

Target delivery hypothesis: five business days after access, scope, and
required evidence are complete.

1. **Qualification:** confirm fit, owner authority, repository boundary, and
   decision objective.
2. **Seal:** record the exact snapshot and prove the review begins from the
   authorized state.
3. **Collect:** run only approved read-only commands and objective diagnostics.
4. **Review:** independently trace the most consequential state and evidence
   chains.
5. **Consolidate:** collapse cascading symptoms into controlling root causes.
6. **Decide:** deliver the few operating decisions that matter and state what
   remains unknown.

The five-day target is a product hypothesis, not an approved customer promise,
until the proof cohort demonstrates delivery capacity.

## Price and commercial boundary

Founding-pilot hypothesis: **`$3,000` fixed fee**.

The price assumes the repository remains inside every stated limit and total
Anyang delivery effort remains at or below eight hours. Remediation,
additional repositories, private-system access, additional reconciliation,
travel, specialist review, and external research require a separate scope and
price.

The founding price is not approved for quotation or sale. Contract, payment,
tax, liability, data-handling, and professional-review requirements remain
separate System Engineer decisions.

## Evidence behind the design

Across three technical benchmarks, the audit method has shown that it can:

- inventory thousands of tracked files without changing the target;
- detect both failing controls and important omissions hidden behind passing
  native tests;
- reconstruct at least 90% of required sampled evidence traces in the two
  external technical benchmarks;
- achieve 100% first-pass precision in deterministic broken-link samples from
  both external benchmarks;
- reduce hundreds or thousands of diagnostics into a small number of
  consequence-ranked root causes;
- preserve independent findings before executive interpretation;
- produce bounded remediation plans without treating findings as repair
  authority.

These are internal benchmark results, not customer outcomes, certifications,
or proof of universal portability. The kernel remains `revise`.

## Internal positioning guardrails

Call the service an **AI Repository Assurance Review**.

Do not call it:

- a complete AI audit;
- a cybersecurity or compliance audit;
- NIST or ISO certification;
- a guarantee that the repository or AI is correct;
- autonomous remediation;
- proof that external claims are true.
