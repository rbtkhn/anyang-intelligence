# Learn From Choices Lite v1

Status: active default interaction contract

Learn From Choices Lite preserves possibility navigation while making durable
learning exceptional and operator-controlled. It replaces automatic
selected-branch retention as the default behavior; it does not remove or
rewrite the schema-v8 choice ledger.

## Default interaction

Every final response still offers three or four meaningful possibilities with
the stable roles `recommended`, `alternative`, `overlooked`, and
`pause-or-deepen`. A letter enters the displayed branch. Reserved first-token
labels `Execute`, `Commit`, `Push`, and `Send` authorize only that named bounded
action, subject to existing authority.

An ordinary selection:

1. enters the selected branch;
2. applies its visible action boundary;
3. performs no choice-status lookup;
4. creates no selection packet or ledger receipt; and
5. causes no retention warning.

Compound and ranked response semantics remain unchanged. Neutral evidence
intake remains governed by Elicitation.

## Persistent learning

`bravo` and `friction` may identify a candidate outcome. They do not persist it
automatically or infer missing cognitive-load, momentum, or discovery values.
Durable repository learning requires a separately displayed and selected
action such as `Execute retain this learning`, plus the existing authority and
privacy boundary.

Only sanitized, operator-approved `RL-*` learning may enter Git. Selection
frequency never supplies learning evidence. After at least five explicit,
comparable, outcome-supported learnings, a read-only review may recommend a
contract change; the operator must separately authorize the change.

## Compatibility surface

SQLite remains schema v8. Existing prompts, events, hashes, corrections,
projections, external-ledger configuration, and `anyang-ops choice` commands
remain readable and usable through explicit invocation. The CLI is an advanced
manual audit surface, not a dependency of ordinary response navigation.

Coffee may read configured choice data to keep recorded authority or membrane
guardrails visible. It does not solicit unresolved outcomes or reserve a menu
slot for choice review under Lite defaults.

Recommendation ordering remains independent of selection frequency and frozen
against the prior calibration pilot until an explicit later disposition says
otherwise. No prior receipt is deleted, rewritten, or backfilled.

## Acceptance

- Ordinary selection causes zero choice-ledger calls.
- Final-response navigation and explicit action boundaries remain intact.
- No outcome or repository learning is persisted automatically.
- Existing schema-v8 data and packets remain compatible.
- A credible overlooked path remains present when one exists.
- No new persistence system or authority effect is introduced.
