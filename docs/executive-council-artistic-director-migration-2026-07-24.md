# Executive Council Artistic Director Migration — 2026-07-24

**Decision ID:** `EC-ARTISTIC-DIRECTOR-2026-07-24-01`

**Approved by:** System Engineer

**Decision:** Add Artistic Director as the fifth durable Executive Council
position and migrate active Media Production direction and production
responsibility from the unfilled Creative Production Operator position.

**State:** `approved structural migration — position vacant and inactive`

## Approved structure

- Artistic Director is a durable human-AI hybrid Council position.
- Media Production remains an Anyang Intelligence commercial service line
  governed by the Artistic Director when the position is separately activated.
- The unfilled Creative Production Operator position is retired through a
  staged, lineage-preserving migration.
- The planned `$500/month` Creative Production Operator allocation becomes
  planned Artistic Director compensation.
- The remaining `$500/month` Media Production budget retains its existing
  production-budget treatment.

## Authority boundary

This migration approves repository governance and operating-model corrections.
It does not:

- appoint a human holder;
- activate an AI runtime;
- authorize artistic production, publication, delivery, spending, payment,
  contracting, employment, hiring, rights clearance, claims, or client action;
- permit the Artistic Director to contact clients or task the Executive
  Assistant;
- transfer client creative authority from a client CEO;
- waive a project membrane or authorize private-system access.

The Artistic Director may begin operating only after a separate activation
receipt names the human holder, AI runtime, sources, tools, permissions,
prohibited evidence, term, review date, persistence boundary, and revocation
path.

## Lineage and retirement

Active Media Production gates, skills, loops, dashboards, and service records
move to the Artistic Director operating model. Historical contractor
onboarding, testing, readiness, ramp, and training artifacts remain in place
with explicit superseded status. Sealed manifests, historical receipts, and
dated source records remain unchanged.

## Receipt model

The existing Executive Council transaction record remains controlling. The
Artistic Director records approved internal ideation or production as the named
executor. The Executive Assistant records real-world communication or delivery
only when invoked. No new Artistic Director receipt family is created.

## Review

After a separately approved activation, measure a 30-day cohort for
ideation-to-shortlist time, concept diversity, strategic fit, first-pass
acceptance, revision rounds, production time, asset reuse, authority and rights
holds, budget variance, and unauthorized action events. Expansion requires a
new System Engineer receipt.
