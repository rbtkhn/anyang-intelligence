# Human Executive Assistant — Role Scorecard

**Status:** Proposed hiring scorecard — not a job posting, offer, employment
agreement, or authority grant

**Prepared:** 2026-08-07

**Position state:** Vacant — human holder not yet hired

**Working title:** Human Executive Assistant and Executive Assistant Agent
Operator

**Reports to:** System Engineer

**Council relationship:** Human operator of the durable Executive Assistant
position within Executive Council

## Mission

Protect executive attention and make approved intent real by operating the
Executive Assistant agent, verifying human-world context, coordinating bounded
work, communicating through approved channels, and returning reliable evidence.

The human does not merely receive AI output. The human supervises, corrects,
and remains accountable for the agent's use within the assigned role.

## Role model

```text
System Engineer authority
  -> Chief Executive prepares a bounded dispatch
  -> human Executive Assistant checks dispatch completeness and existing authorization
  -> Executive Assistant agent helps prepare and coordinate
  -> human Executive Assistant reviews and executes approved action
  -> evidence and exceptions return
```

The agent does not replace the human holder. The human holder does not gain
company authority merely by operating the agent.

## Outcomes

The person is succeeding when:

1. Executive commitments, decisions, deadlines, and follow-ups are visible and
   reliably reconciled.
2. The Executive Assistant agent reduces net cognitive load rather than
   creating another review burden.
3. External communication and coordination occur only under clear authority.
4. Real-world facts, changed circumstances, and exceptions return promptly.
5. Material actions have complete, source-linked receipts.
6. Private information, credentials, and sensitive evidence remain in approved
   systems.
7. The person confidently stops or escalates ambiguous, unsafe, unauthorized,
   or materially changed work.
8. The agent improves through explicit human corrections without silently
   expanding its permissions.

## Core responsibilities

### Executive operations

- Maintain the approved operating picture, calendar, obligations, decisions,
  deadlines, and follow-ups.
- Prepare and review morning briefs, meeting packets, commitment records, and
  end-of-day reconciliations.
- Batch routine matters and surface only consequential exceptions.
- Clarify owners, due dates, evidence requirements, and next decisions.

### Agent operation and supervision

- Operate the Executive Assistant agent through approved tools and accounts.
- Review AI-generated briefs, messages, plans, research, and proposed actions
  before reliance or transmission.
- Correct factual mistakes, overconfidence, scope drift, and weak assumptions.
- Keep agent memory within approved privacy and persistence boundaries.
- Report tool failures, hallucinations, repeated friction, and workflow risks.
- Propose bounded workflow improvements for separate approval.

### Communication and coordination

- Serve as Executive Council's sole normal human interface with external
  parties.
- Send only approved communications to named recipients through approved
  channels.
- Coordinate appointments, vendors, collaborators, clients, and account setup
  within exact limits.
- Preserve the difference between a request, acknowledgment, response,
  agreement, approval, and completed action.

### Execution and evidence

- Execute approved purchases, filings, account actions, scheduling, and other
  operational work only within the named authority and spending ceiling.
- Return receipts, confirmations, costs, commitments, deviations, and follow-up
  requirements.
- Store sensitive evidence outside Git in its approved private location.
- Escalate unexpected fees, recurring commitments, changed terms, privacy
  exposure, unavailable names, conflicting ownership, or new legal questions.

## Authority boundary

The human Executive Assistant may:

- observe approved sources;
- advise and ask clarifying questions;
- draft internal and external material for review;
- coordinate work under a valid dispatch;
- execute reversible or consequential actions only when the exact authority
  permits them;
- decline, block, stop, or escalate work that lacks sufficient authority or
  evidence; and
- return evidence without treating the receipt as a new approval.

The human Executive Assistant may not independently:

- approve spending, contracts, hiring, publication, legal positions, company
  policy, client decisions, or sensitive-data access;
- infer permission from urgency, access, prior practice, silence, or personal
  confidence;
- allow the agent to send messages or operate external systems without the
  required review and authorization;
- disclose credentials, private records, customer information, or protected
  project context;
- make Grace Gems, Grace Mar, Learning Core, Mountain Villa, or another
  project's decisions on its owner's behalf;
- task the Artistic Director directly outside the approved Council flow; or
- create a substitute external interface when unavailable without a named,
  time-bounded System Engineer exception.

## Required capabilities

- Strong executive-assistance or operations judgment.
- Clear, concise written and verbal communication.
- Ability to organize ambiguous work into owners, decisions, deadlines, and
  evidence.
- Practical fluency with generative AI systems and willingness to inspect,
  correct, and challenge their output.
- Sound privacy, credential, and account-security habits.
- Comfort learning new software, administrative systems, and lightweight
  repository workflows.
- Ability to distinguish recommendation from authority and activity from
  completion.
- Calm escalation and refusal when scope, evidence, safety, ownership, or
  authority is unclear.
- High reliability with confidential and consequential work.

## Preferred capabilities

- Experience supporting a founder, owner, executive, or small operating team.
- Experience with Google Workspace, calendars, shared documents, domain and
  account administration, and social-platform operations.
- Experience coordinating company formation, bookkeeping, vendors, or legal
  and tax professionals without substituting for professional judgment.
- Basic Git and GitHub literacy sufficient to inspect status, review documents,
  and preserve repository boundaries.
- Familiarity with AI-assisted operating workflows, prompt refinement, quality
  control, automation supervision, or evidence-based task management.
- Experience serving clients or handling high-trust personal and business
  information.

## Evaluation scorecard

Score each dimension from `1` to `5`. A high total does not override a material
authority, integrity, privacy, or judgment concern.

| Dimension | Weight | Evidence sought |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Executive operations and follow-through | 20% | Maintains commitments, deadlines, owners, and closure evidence |
| Judgment and escalation | 20% | Stops appropriately; identifies changed scope, risk, and missing authority |
| AI supervision | 15% | Directs, verifies, corrects, and improves agent output without overreliance |
| Communication | 15% | Produces concise, accurate, audience-appropriate messages and briefs |
| Privacy and security | 15% | Handles credentials, private data, recovery, and access conservatively |
| Evidence discipline | 10% | Distinguishes facts, reports, assumptions, approvals, actions, and receipts |
| Tool learning and adaptability | 5% | Learns approved systems without uncontrolled experimentation |

### Non-compensable concerns

Do not hire merely because the candidate is fast, agreeable, available, or
technically fluent if the candidate:

- treats access as authority;
- sends AI output without review;
- conceals errors or deviations;
- handles credentials casually;
- cannot say `stop`, `missing`, or `I need approval`;
- creates commitments to appear helpful;
- over-collects personal information; or
- cannot distinguish a polished artifact from reliable evidence.

## Work-sample evaluation

Use a sanitized, fictional packet. Do not provide real credentials, private
customer information, or live external access during selection.

Ask the candidate to:

1. Review a morning brief containing five obligations and identify the top
   three priorities.
2. Find the missing authority fields in a proposed external dispatch.
3. Use an AI assistant to draft a concise stakeholder message.
4. Critique and correct the AI draft.
5. Convert a simulated response into decisions, unknowns, commitments, and a
   receipt.
6. Explain what they would refuse, hold, or escalate and why.

Evaluate the reasoning and corrections, not merely the polish of the final
message.

## Interview questions

- Tell us about a time you stopped or escalated an executive request because
  an important fact or authorization was missing.
- How do you verify AI-generated information before acting on it?
- What information should never be placed in an AI chat, repository, or shared
  task system?
- How would you handle an urgent request to spend money when the owner is
  unavailable?
- How do you prevent reminders and dashboards from becoming additional work?
- What would you need to know before creating a business account or sending a
  message in the company's name?
- How would you correct an agent that repeatedly confuses a draft with an
  approval?
- Which parts of your working style should an assistant agent learn, and which
  should remain private?

## Initial 90-day path

### Days 1–30 — orientation and shadow mode

- Complete role, privacy, security, agent, and authority onboarding.
- Review outputs without external execution.
- Learn the portfolio, communication protocol, receipt system, and stop rules.
- Establish approved working preferences and memory boundaries.
- Demonstrate accurate triage and correction using simulated or internal work.

### Days 31–60 — bounded operation

- Coordinate approved internal workflows.
- Prepare and send narrowly approved communications.
- Return evidence through the standard receipt.
- Operate selected account or administrative lanes with explicit limits.
- Record agent corrections and review burden.

### Days 61–90 — reviewed expansion

- Expand only into named lanes supported by pilot evidence.
- Demonstrate reliable recurring cadences and lower executive cognitive load.
- Complete a formal authority, access, security, and usefulness review.
- Retain, narrow, expand, or end the engagement through an explicit decision.

## Pilot success measures

Measure:

- percentage of material actions with complete receipts;
- clarification and rework rate;
- executive hours saved per week;
- Executive Assistant review burden;
- missed and recovered commitments;
- avoidable interruptions and false-positive alerts;
- escalation precision;
- agent corrections by workflow;
- privacy, security, authority, and client-boundary incidents; and
- usefulness ratings from the System Engineer and human Executive Assistant.

Do not expand delegated authority unless the pilot demonstrates at least one
meaningful efficiency improvement, reliable evidence return, and zero material
authority or client-boundary violations.

## Activation after hiring

Selection, engagement, role onboarding, agent-runtime activation, access, and
delegated authority are separate events.

After hiring:

1. The human reviews and corrects the proposed role and working preferences.
2. The System Engineer approves the engagement and applicable authority.
3. Access is granted minimally and recorded separately.
4. The Executive Assistant agent receives its own runtime, tool, persistence,
   review, recovery, and revocation boundaries.
5. The pilot begins in shadow mode.

No candidate, employee, contractor, or agent receives external-action authority
through title, access, hiring, onboarding, or proximity alone.
