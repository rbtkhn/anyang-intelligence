## Term and review

If adopted, the interface remains effective until held, corrected, revoked, or
superseded by the System Engineer.

### Scheduled review

Review quarterly. If adopted on 2026-08-05, the first scheduled review is
2026-11-05.

The quarterly review examines only:

- material changes since the prior review;
- unresolved holds, incidents, contradictions, and aging obligations;
- a bounded sample of consequential or cross-project handoffs;
- standing-mandate state, if any;
- downstream outcomes, including `caused-unintended-work`;
- validation failures and stale references; and
- whether review burden remains proportionate to prevented authority,
  membrane, or state-lineage errors.

Routine archive growth, source volume, analysis count, and rhetorical novelty
are not review triggers.

### Event-driven review

Review before the next affected use when:

- the Executive Council authority envelope, role structure, delegation rules,
  or runtime model changes;
- a Singularity Science state namespace, governed skill, archive lane, source
  class, or access rule changes;
- a new receiving lane is introduced;
- a receiving-lane authority or membrane changes;
- a standing mandate is created, expanded, renewed, or transferred; or
- a new external, commercial, publication, private-source, or protected-action
  pathway is proposed.

Immediately hold affected work and review when:

- private or protected context crosses a membrane without authority;
- research language is treated as approval, adoption, or professional advice;
- source provenance, rights, state lineage, or decision ownership becomes
  materially ambiguous;
- testing, persistence, access, spending, publication, or implementation begins
  without matching authority;
- an unsupported completion or adoption claim appears; or
- downstream evidence reports material unintended work.

### Review output

Every review returns exactly one disposition:

- `continue unchanged`;
- `correct exact defect`;
- `hold affected pathway`;
- `supersede contract`; or
- `revoke interface`.

The review records the controlling version, evidence examined, unresolved
items, decision authority required, and next review date. Review does not
automatically expand authority, renew a standing mandate, or adopt research.

## Revocation and recovery

The System Engineer may hold or revoke the interface immediately.

Revocation:

- preserves this receipt and historical research lineage;
- removes reliance on this interface for new tasking;
- creates no retroactive authority;
- returns tasking to the controlling [Executive Council role
  contract](executive-council-role-contract.md) and governed [Singularity
  Science skill](../skills/singularity-science/SKILL.md); and
- requires open work to stop or obtain replacement authority.

Recovery requires naming affected tasks, preserving returned evidence, marking
stale references, and adopting a corrected or superseding contract.
