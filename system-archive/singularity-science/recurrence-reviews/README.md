# Singularity Science Recurrence Reviews

This shelf stores derived recurrence-review packets for completed Singularity
Science intakes.

Recurrence reviews compare source notes, analyses, and ledgers across prior
archive material to identify independent recurring seams, contradictions,
evidence gaps, and next-review triggers. They are archive-learning artifacts,
not source packets, canonical ledgers, doctrine, or customer-facing claims.

Use `singularity-recurrence-review` for interpretive recurrence work. Use
`archive-steward` only for shelf structure, manifest alignment, stale paths,
tombstones, or archive-readiness checks.

## Boundary

Recurrence reviews may inform later learning updates or primitive-candidate
review, but they do not by themselves authorize belief, doctrine promotion,
customer action, public publication, or transcript reuse.
