Why it matters:

The episode compresses twelve practitioner lessons into a coherent operating sequence: identify the business constraint, define an outcome, supply domain context, manage the agent toward an explicit finish line, restrict its capabilities, and verify performance over repeated runs. Its strongest value is the linkage between business evidence and technical governance rather than any individual tool recommendation.

Dominant seams:
- Outcome receipts and a predeclared North Star replace portfolio claims with baseline-to-result evidence.
- Tool-agnostic expertise and context engineering encode domain judgment, including lessons learned from failure.
- Managed orchestration uses clarification, adversarial review, delegation, and an explicit definition of done.
- Verification, evaluations, and tool-level permissioning form a defense-in-depth production gate.
- Cost-aware model routing matches task demands to the least expensive model that can meet the evidence threshold.
