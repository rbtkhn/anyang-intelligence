# Media Production Risk Register

This register tracks the main operating and governance risks for Media Production.

Review cadence: weekly operating review and before any unusual spend,
activation, production, staffing action, or publication-sensitive delivery.

| Risk | Consequence | Owner | Current posture | Next review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Artistic Director ideation or production begins without holder acceptance, runtime activation, and an approved brief | Authority blurs, rework increases, and client trust falls | System Engineer | Keep the appointed-but-unaccepted position inactive; use the artistic production gate before persistent ideation or production | Weekly operating review |
| Executive Assistant and Artistic Director modes are collapsed in one action | Creative execution could silently become external delivery or self-approval | System Engineer | Require explicit mode labels, separate dispatch authority, separate evidence, and Council Steward sampling | Every dual-role transaction |
| Rights or factual uncertainty is missed before publication | Published assets may create claim, attribution, or legal risk | Anyang Intelligence operator | Keep quality gate and rights review explicit before publication | Before each publish-ready asset |
| Grace Gems work crowds out Predictive History or vice versa | Production becomes reactive and loses strategic coherence | Anyang Intelligence operator | Use lane-aware backlog and calendar review | Weekly operating review |
| The $1,000 operating ceiling is treated as allocated cash or spent without clear abundance value | Small-budget production capacity gets exhausted with low learning return or unauthorized commitments | System Engineer | Keep allocation at `$0` until exact compensation or operating costs receive separate approval; review approved spend against reuse and output quality | Weekly budget review |
| Artistic Director capacity is assumed before activation or measurement | Deadlines, quality, and review discipline degrade | Chief Executive | Treat capacity as zero until activation; default to the smallest useful scope afterward | Before new production set |
| The Artistic Director operating ceiling is treated as compensation or payment authority | Unauthorized contract, employment, tax, or payment exposure | System Engineer | Do not pre-classify the ceiling; require a separate agreement, allocation, and financial approval | Before staffing or payment |
