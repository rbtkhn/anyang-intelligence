# Media Production Risk Register

This register tracks the main operating and governance risks for Media Production.

Review cadence: weekly operating review and before any unusual spend,
activation, production, staffing action, or publication-sensitive delivery.

| Risk | Consequence | Owner | Current posture | Next review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Artistic Director ideation or production begins without activation and an approved brief | Authority blurs, rework increases, and client trust falls | System Engineer | Keep the vacant position inactive; use the artistic production gate before persistent ideation or production | Weekly operating review |
| Rights or factual uncertainty is missed before publication | Published assets may create claim, attribution, or legal risk | Anyang Intelligence operator | Keep quality gate and rights review explicit before publication | Before each publish-ready asset |
| Grace Gems work crowds out Predictive History or vice versa | Production becomes reactive and loses strategic coherence | Anyang Intelligence operator | Use lane-aware backlog and calendar review | Weekly operating review |
| The $500 discretionary budget is spent without clear abundance value | Small-budget production capacity gets exhausted with low learning return | Anyang Intelligence operator | Review spend against expected asset reuse and output quality | Weekly budget review |
| Artistic Director capacity is assumed before activation or measurement | Deadlines, quality, and review discipline degrade | Chief Executive | Treat capacity as zero until activation; default to the smallest useful scope afterward | Before new production set |
| Planned Artistic Director compensation is treated as payment authority | Unauthorized contract, employment, tax, or payment exposure | System Engineer | Preserve the allocation as uncommitted until a separate agreement and financial approval exist | Before staffing or payment |
