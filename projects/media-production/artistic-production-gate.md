# Artistic Production Gate

Use this gate before the Artistic Director persists an ideation output, begins
production, or consumes material capacity.

**Position state:** `interim holder appointed; acceptance and runtime
activation pending — no production may begin until a separate activation and
task approval are current`

This gate protects source truth, project membranes, rights, claims, capacity,
client authority, and budget. It does not approve publication, external
delivery, spending, rights clearance, client commitments, or claims.

## Decision

Choose one:

- `Proceed`: the approved brief is source-bound, capacity-bounded, and ready
  for the named internal ideation or production action.
- `Revise brief`: the concept is useful but its source, scope, output, or
  evidence requirement needs tightening.
- `Hold`: authority, source, rights, claims, capacity, client context, or budget
  remains incomplete.
- `Reject`: the work is not worth producing in its current form.

An idea does not enter production automatically. When authority, source,
rights, or budget is missing, choose `Hold`.

`Proceed` authorizes only the disposition and internal action named in the
approved brief. A recommendation, shortlist, or selected direction does not
by itself authorize production.

## Required inputs

- Task ID and approved objective.
- Lane and source boundary.
- Audience and problem.
- Human decision owner and that person's stated intent.
- Facts, mechanics, or constraints that must remain fixed.
- Three to five materially different directions when ideation is requested.
- A meaningful preserved alternative when a recommendation is requested.
- Selected direction and rationale when production is requested.
- Exact permitted output and disposition:
  `explore / shortlist / brief / hold / reject`.
- Do-not-invent list.
- Claims, rights, privacy, and representation risks.
- Material-capacity and budget limit.
- Required evidence and reviewer.
- Smallest useful next proof and next decision owner.
- Expiry, stop conditions, and recovery path.
- Applicable client authority when client adoption or use is proposed.

## Lane checks

### Grace Gems

- Bind concepts to approved product and business facts.
- Do not invent materials, gemstone identity, authenticity, price, dimensions,
  personalization, shipping, returns, repairs, certificates, or customer
  promises.
- Treat product visualization as a concept unless the Grace Gems CEO approves
  the represented product and customer-facing use.

### Predictive History and editorial work

- Bind concepts to the episode, source note, argument, or approved topic.
- Make judgment, evidence, prediction, outcome, causal forces, or uncertainty
  easier to understand.
- Hold sensational or decorative concepts that distort the source.
- Flag quotes, maps, likenesses, images, music, and source rights.

### Learning Core

- Name the learner, concept, and educational objective.
- Keep child safety, accessibility, pedagogy, and parent authority outside
  Artistic Director approval.

### Mountain Villa and other portfolio lanes

- Use only approved lane context.
- Compare abstract creative patterns without transferring protected project
  details.
- Preserve owner authority over property representation and external use.

## Capacity and budget

- Confirm that the position is activated and the exact task is approved.
- Size the first production action to the smallest useful output.
- Treat the `$1,000/month` Artistic Director operating envelope as a ceiling,
  not compensation, allocation, or spend authority.
- Flag paid tools, assets, contractors, distribution, or rush work before use.
- Require separate System Engineer approval before spend or commitment.

## Evidence return

The Artistic Director returns:

- concept set or produced asset;
- sources used;
- selected direction and rationale;
- meaningful preserved alternative, when a recommendation was requested;
- confirmation of the facts, mechanics, or constraints left unchanged;
- claims, rights, privacy, or representation uncertainties;
- production time and material capacity used;
- deviations from the brief;
- required next decision;
- smallest useful next proof;
- reuse or repurposing opportunities; and
- transaction-record action ID.

`Artistically review-ready` is not `approved`, `delivered`, or `published`.
