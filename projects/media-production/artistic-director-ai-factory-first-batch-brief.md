## Creative thesis

Working thesis: **A small visual world becomes emotionally real when its recurring people, places, and rules remain coherent while the camera discovers new meaning in them.**

The batch should test whether a compact asset system can produce work that feels alive rather than merely consistent.

## Style-bible draft

Editorial animation language: expressive 2D illustration with deliberate line weight, restrained but emotionally responsive color, clear silhouettes, tactile environmental detail, and camera movement that reveals character intention. Favor designed composition and readable emotional beats over visual noise, generic cinematic gloss, or photorealism.

Do not imitate a named studio, living artist, franchise, copyrighted character, or recognizable brand language. Treat this as a provisional internal direction, not approved canon.

## Character reference directions

### Character 1 — The Keeper

An observant young archivist who carries a small fold-out lantern and notices details others overlook. Design questions: how can posture, gaze, and the lantern communicate care without sentimentality?

### Character 2 — The Improviser

A restless mapmaker who redraws routes whenever the world changes. Design questions: how can movement, asymmetry, and tool choice communicate curiosity without becoming comic noise?

Both are fictional internal concepts. No likeness, real person, cultural insignia, or protected character should be introduced.

## Location reference directions

### Location 1 — The Moving Archive

A compact archive room whose drawers, labels, windows, and worktable create a repeatable geography. The room should support quiet observation, discovery, and a sudden change in light.

### Location 2 — The Unfinished Map Room

A high room with suspended paper maps, floor markings, and one open wall to an unknown landscape. It should support disagreement, movement, and a clear visual sense of choices not yet made.

## Voice direction

Two contrasting internal voice directions:

- The Keeper: calm, precise, warm without theatricality.
- The Improviser: quick, questioning, energetic without caricature.

No voice cloning, recording, or rendering is authorized by this brief.

## Three scene / shot concepts

1. **The missing drawer:** The Keeper finds a blank space in the archive; the Improviser insists the missing record may be a route rather than a loss. Visual test: can silence and small gestures carry the scene?
2. **The map that moves:** A map changes as the characters disagree about where the boundary belongs. Visual test: can the asset system show conceptual movement without relying on spectacle?
3. **The lantern test:** The Keeper lights the lantern and reveals that the archive has been arranged around an unseen audience. Visual test: can lighting shift meaning while preserving the same location canon?

## Proposed selection and rejection

- Proposed direction to shortlist: `The missing drawer` because it tests emotional restraint, character distinction, and repeatable archive geography.
- Proposed direction to reject for this batch: `The map that moves` because its current concept depends on animation behavior that has not yet been defined in the asset or continuity system. Rejection is provisional and is not a judgment against future development.
