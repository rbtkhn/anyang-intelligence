## Objective

Prepare one coherent, reusable visual system for a bounded Grace Gems
fine-jewelry proof across the proposed owned website and independently acquired
marketing channels.

Graphic design is the scope of this brief. Live website engineering,
e-commerce configuration, publication, distribution, and business changes are
outside scope.

## Required decisions before ideation

The Grace Gems CEO must identify:

- the initial owned-channel brand;
- one fine-jewelry collection;
- the intended customer and customer promise;
- the products and approved source facts in scope;
- the creative decision owner and review cadence; and
- claims, imagery, privacy, and representation restrictions.

Until those decisions and the Artistic Director activation are current, no
persistent concept or production asset may be created.

## Source boundary

### Permitted after approval

- CEO-selected collection and product facts.
- Approved public shop and product sources.
- Claim-approved trust and policy language.
- Existing Grace Gems visual assets with confirmed reuse rights.
- Sanitized customer-confusion patterns returned through an approved evidence
  process.

### Excluded

- Raw customer, order, supplier, financial, analytics, or private operational
  records.
- Unapproved product, origin, authenticity, material, shipping,
  personalization, certificate, return, repair, price, or availability claims.
- Third-party images, fonts, music, likenesses, or design assets without
  confirmed rights.

## Phase 1 — direction set

After activation and a separate Class 1 task approval, produce three to five
materially different directions. Each direction must include:

- concept and buyer-facing mechanism;
- color, typography, spacing, and image-treatment approach;
- homepage, collection, and product-page visual implications;
- Pinterest, social, and email adaptation;
- trust-architecture treatment;
- strategic fit and differentiation;
- claim, rights, accessibility, and representation risks;
- estimated production effort and reuse potential; and
- disposition: `explore / shortlist / brief / hold / reject`.

The Chief Executive tests strategic fit. The System Engineer selects the
internal shortlist. The Grace Gems CEO selects or rejects the client direction.

## Phase 2 — bounded production set

Only after a direction is selected and separately approved, prepare:

1. a lightweight visual system covering color, typography, spacing, image
   treatment, icon or badge behavior, and mobile readability;
2. owner-reviewable homepage, collection-page, and product-page design
   concepts;
3. reusable product-image and trust-graphic templates;
4. a compact marketing template family for Pinterest, social, and voluntary
   email;
5. a Trust Stack one-pager and Custom Order Journey graphic; and
6. an implementation handoff describing file formats, dimensions,
   accessibility needs, claim dependencies, and what remains unapproved.

These are review assets, not a live website or approved campaign.

- client-supplied assets have approved reuse rights;
- the system feels premium, calm, legible, mobile-ready, and accessible;

delivery, website implementation, publication, marketing distribution, or
