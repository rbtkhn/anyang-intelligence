### Trademark and name risk

This packet does not provide trademark clearance. A broad preliminary USPTO
wordmark query is not enough to determine registrability or infringement risk,
and the terms `Grace` and `Mar` appear in numerous live and dead records,
including jewelry-related Class 014 records. Clearance should cover exact and
similar marks for `Grace Mar` and `Grace Gems`, jewelry and retail services,
state records, domains, marketplaces, social use, and common-law sources. The
existing Colorado `Grace Gems LLC` makes the ownership inquiry mandatory even
if no exact federal registration is found.

### Administration and security gate

- Name a primary admin and a distinct recovery admin; shared `admin@` logins are
  not proposed.
- Require phishing-resistant 2-step verification for both admins: two hardware
  security keys or passkeys per person where supported, with an authenticator
  app as controlled fallback. Do not use SMS as the primary method.
- Store backup/recovery codes only in the approved private vault; never in Git,
  Telegram, email drafts, or the social group archive.
- Use least-privilege delegated roles for normal work and reserve super-admin
  use for domain, billing, recovery, and high-risk changes.
- Privately confirm registrar control, current DNS, administrative/technical
  contacts, expiry, auto-renewal, payment owner, and recovery before Workspace
  verification.
- The later approved DNS plan should include domain verification, Google MX,
  SPF, DKIM, and DMARC staged with monitoring. This packet authorizes none of
  those changes.

Official source: [Google 2-Step Verification guidance](https://support.google.com/a/answer/175197).
