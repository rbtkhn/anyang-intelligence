## Interpretation

The method shows strong boundary compatibility but no demonstrated operational ROI yet. The only supported claim is that a shared review structure can be applied across lanes without automatically transferring lane-specific authority or private context.

The two-artifact-per-lane review expanded the sample to 10 artifacts across five lanes. All 10 exposed a visible evidence/review loop, canonical human authority, and a stop/hold boundary; zero automatic private-context or lane-authority transfers were observed. See [Two-Artifact-Per-Lane Membrane Review](cross-lane-two-artifacts-per-lane-result.md).

## Minimum proof for operational benefit

To establish value, run the same checklist on at least two comparable non-executing artifacts per lane and record:

- reviewer minutes before and after the shared checklist;
- number and severity of authority or membrane corrections;
- evidence gaps found before and after reuse;
- repeated-work or rediscovery avoided;
- whether a later approved artifact reused the method;
- any new burden introduced by the shared fields.

Do not count cross-lane applicability alone as value. Promotion requires measured improvement that exceeds the added review burden and preserves each lane's authority.
