## Findings

- Learning Core preserves observation, interpretation, continuity, and parent authority; transfer remains held until the artifact, evidence, and authority receipt are named.
- Media Production preserves model, quality, rights, cost, and approval boundaries; independent comparison and reproducibility remain held.
- Grace Gems preserves meaningful owner approval at customer-promise boundaries; private customer, supplier, product, pricing, and publication matters remain local.
- Mountain Villa permits risk ranking and proof planning; safety, property access, contractor, insurance, spending, and professional judgment remain held.
- Book Club permits cadence, memory, and discussion support; member identity, donor restrictions, participation, tone, and community norms remain local.

## Decision

The shared method has structural compatibility across all five lanes, but this is not proof of operational benefit. Retain the cross-lane learning-loop method as a `testable-primitive` and require timed, repeated, non-executing artifact reviews before promotion.

No lane artifacts were mutated. No customer, child, member, property, publication, spend, or external communication authority was created.
