## Shared method

The cross-lane method remains portable:

`evidence → bounded recommendation → human review → observed outcome → correction → approved reuse`

## Membrane findings

- Grace Gems requires the strongest customer-trust boundary: private customer, order, supplier, and financial records cannot become reusable guidance without explicit review.
- Mountain Villa requires the strongest physical-world boundary: readiness planning cannot become property access, safety, contractor, insurance, or emergency authority.
- Book Club requires the strongest participation boundary: donor support and shared materials cannot make participation paid, ranked, surveilled, or transactional.
- Generic review methods may transfer; lane-specific facts, judgments, and authority do not.

## Decision

`Review exercise only`.

The membrane rules generalize across three more lanes without weakening their canonical authorities. No lane artifact was changed, no private context was transferred, and no execution, communication, spending, or customer/member action was authorized.

## Remaining gap

This confirms boundary fit, not operational value. No lane has yet supplied comparable measured cleanup, cost, or repeated-cycle evidence for cross-lane learning-loop reuse.
