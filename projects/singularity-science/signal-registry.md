# Singularity Science Signal Registry

This registry links sources into recurring signals, reusable mechanisms, and primitive tests without duplicating source material.

## Identifier rules

- `source_id` identifies one landed source packet.
- `cluster_id` groups sources examining the same signal.
- `mechanism_id` identifies a reusable operating mechanism.
- `primitive_id` identifies a tested or preserved primitive.

## Promotion rules

- A source may enter a cluster when its signal is materially related to another source.
- A cluster becomes a recurring signal only after independent corroboration or repeated observation.
- A recurring signal becomes a reusable mechanism only when the abstraction is source-independent.
- A mechanism becomes a primitive candidate only when it can be expressed as a checklist, gate, validator, template, or operating method.
- A primitive becomes tested or adopted only through a human-reviewed receipt.

## Boundary

The registry tracks research relationships and uncertainty. It does not establish source truth, rights clearance, customer authority, or operating doctrine.
