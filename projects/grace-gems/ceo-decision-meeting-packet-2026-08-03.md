### 5. Decide the custom-order vocabulary

> The term "custom" currently covers several different types of work. We
> propose an internal vocabulary so each request can receive the right
> approval, price, timing, and risk controls.

- **Personalization:** adding a personal detail without changing the main
  design.
- **Configuration:** choosing permitted options for an existing design.
- **Made to order:** producing an existing design after purchase.
- **Bespoke commission:** creating a new or substantially redesigned piece.

`Standard ready-to-order` remains the reference classification for an
unchanged existing design without personalization; it is not one of the four
proposed custom-order categories.
