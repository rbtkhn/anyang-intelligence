# Game Design 30-Day Apprenticeship Installation Plan

> **Status:** Readiness plan. It does not activate a game, authorize production,
> or impose a deadline.

## Week 1: Experience And Mechanics Literacy

- Complete the First Activation Gate for one learning question.
- Define an intended player experience in Robert's words.
- Learn the difference between theme, rule, mechanic, system, feedback, and
  player consequence.
- Compare examples and counterexamples without selecting a product direction.
- Complete one bounded thought exercise.

Evidence: one question, one concept explanation, Robert's articulation, and one
open question.

## Week 2: Mechanic Hypotheses

- Generate three materially different mechanic alternatives.
- Predict how each may shape player behavior or feeling.
- Select one hypothesis through Robert's creative judgment.
- Define deliberate omissions and a revision threshold.
- Prepare a rules sketch, cards, tokens, or decision table.

Evidence: alternatives, selection rationale, mechanic hypothesis, and smallest
prototype specification.

## Week 3: Prototype And Observation

- Create or run only the separately authorized lowest-fidelity prototype.
- Record what happened separately from why it may have happened.
- Preserve denominators, contradictions, and unknowns.
- Review accessibility, rights, privacy, safety, and manipulation boundaries.
- Continue, revise, or hold based on the stated evidence threshold.

Evidence: prototype artifact or rules, observation record, boundary check, and
decision disposition.

## Week 4: Critique And Reflection

- Trace one consequential causal chain from rule to player consequence.
- Identify the strongest property and the highest-leverage seam.
- Propose and, if authorized, test one revision.
- Separate a candidate human lesson from a candidate agent lesson.
- Decide whether another learning cycle is justified.

Evidence: critique, revision hypothesis, comparison, and explicit lesson state.

## 30-Day Review

Ask:

- Is Robert's causal design reasoning clearer?
- Did the agent teach without stealing authorship?
- Did prototypes remain smaller than the ideas they tested?
- Were claims matched to evidence?
- Did any implementation, publication, spending, rights, privacy, safety, or
  cross-lane boundary drift?
- Is the next cycle justified, or should the lane remain held?

Completing the plan does not establish professional status, commercial
readiness, product-market fit, or publication authority.
