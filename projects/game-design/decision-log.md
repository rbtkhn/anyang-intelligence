## Decision Discipline

- Preserve Robert's wording for the intended experience.
- Distinguish Robert preference, agent recommendation, hypothesis, observation,
  and accepted lesson.
- Link the decision to the smallest experiment capable of changing it.
- Record what remains unauthorized.
- Do not interpret silence or repeated selection as durable preference.
