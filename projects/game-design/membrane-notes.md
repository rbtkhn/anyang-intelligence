# Game Design Membrane Notes

Use [../../docs/membranes.md](../../docs/membranes.md) before transferring any
fact, lesson, asset, claim, template, or instruction into or out of Game Design.

## Core Rule

Transfer primitives, not private context. The existence of this lane does not
make facts from another project available to it, and a useful game-design
pattern does not authorize action in another lane.

## Shareable Primitives

After translation and evidence review, these may cross:

- meaningful-choice questions;
- mechanic-hypothesis formats;
- low-fidelity prototype patterns;
- observation-versus-interpretation discipline;
- critique questions and causal-chain methods;
- accessibility, rights, safety, and manipulation-risk checklists;
- evidence thresholds and hold conditions;
- generic learning-loop improvements.

## Keep Local Unless Approved

- Robert's unaccepted preferences or subjective reports;
- unpublished game concepts, rules, narrative, or strategy;
- prototype recordings, transcripts, identities, telemetry, or psychological
  interpretations;
- another participant's feedback or personal information;
- customer, family, learner, donor, property, community, contractor, or
  employee facts;
- private pricing, budgets, product plans, commercial strategy, or performance;
- client assets, rights-sensitive sources, and unapproved external claims;
- child-facing observations or educational claims.

## Adjacent Lane Boundaries

### Media Production

Game Design may supply an approved player-experience or mechanic brief. Media
Production separately owns approved visual, narrative, audio, video, and
promotional asset production. Client assets and strategy do not transfer back.

### System Engineering

Game Design may supply an implementation-ready design brief. System Engineering
separately owns architecture, code, infrastructure, security, and deployment.
Technical feasibility does not silently choose the player experience.

### Singularity Science

Only source-linked, translated research primitives may enter. Transcript bulk,
speculation, and untested claims do not become design doctrine.

### Learning Core

Only generic interaction or motivation primitives may be considered, after a
separate child-safety and educational-evidence review. Learner and family facts
never cross. Child-facing work requires parent or guardian authority.

### Customer And External Work

No customer fact, private message, campaign, asset, approval, or authority
decision enters Game Design by default. A game-design concept does not authorize
publication, distribution, external playtesting, or customer action.

## Required Transfer Receipt

Before a transfer, record:

```text
Source lane:
Receiving lane:
Transfer candidate:
Evidence:
Sensitivity:
Safe transformed version:
Human authority:
Disposition: safe to transfer / translate first / approval required / hold
```

When provenance, consent, evidence, sensitivity, or authority is uncertain,
record `hold`.
