# Game Design Risk Register

The lane is currently held and inactive. Ratings remain provisional until an
exact design question and decision owner activate a bounded cycle.

| Risk | Area | Current posture | Owner | Mitigation and evidence | Review trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Premature production | Scope | Hold | Robert | Test the design question at the lowest useful fidelity before considering code or assets | A prototype proposal requires implementation |
| Agent steals authorship | Creative authority | Guardrail active | Robert | Preserve meaningful alternatives and record Robert's intention separately from agent recommendations | The agent presents one aesthetic as inevitable |
| Imagined fun becomes fact | Evidence | Guardrail active | Robert | Label claims as hypotheses until observed play evidence exists | “Fun,” “balanced,” or “clear” is asserted without evidence |
| Preference bubble | Learning | Guardrail active | Robert | Do not infer permanent taste from repeated choices or selection frequency | Options narrow because of prior selections |
| Scope inflation | Operations | Hold | Robert | Keep one question, one hypothesis, and one smallest useful prototype per cycle | A lesson expands into a curriculum or product build |
| Manipulative monetization | Ethics | Hold | Human authority | Require explicit design, safety, and authority review before gambling-like or exploitative systems | Monetization affects behavior or vulnerable players |
| Rights or provenance failure | Rights | Hold | Human authority | Record source and rights basis before using external material | Licensed, copied, or unclear-rights material is proposed |
| Accessibility exclusion | Player experience | Review required | Robert | Name relevant access assumptions and testable barriers without claiming universal coverage | Audience or interface requirements are selected |
| Unsafe external playtest | Privacy and safety | Hold | Human authority | Define consent, identity, retention, data, and supervision boundaries first | Another participant is invited |
| Child-facing work without authority | Child safety | Hold | Parent or guardian | Require parent or guardian approval plus explicit privacy, supervision, and evidence rules | A child audience or learner context is proposed |
| Cross-lane context leak | Membrane | Guardrail active | Council Steward | Transfer generic primitives only after membrane review | Another project supplies facts, assets, or private context |
| Unsupported implementation | Technical authority | Hold | System Engineer and Robert | Require a design brief and separate implementation decision | Code, engine, architecture, or deployment is proposed |

## Escalation Rule

Rights, privacy, child safety, external participation, spending, publication,
manipulative monetization, and unsupported implementation risks produce `hold`
until the named human authority resolves the boundary.
