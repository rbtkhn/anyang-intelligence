## Boundary

This receipt records provenance, review evidence, and the repository operator's
adoption decision. It does not authorize publication, deployment, spending,
customer contact, private-data access, installation elsewhere, or execution of
scripts and tools. Missing source identity, unresolved rights, opaque
executable behavior, unbounded permissions, or no credible rollback requires
`Hold`.
